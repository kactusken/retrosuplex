## Why wrestling games

Japanese pro wrestling games from the 90s and 2000s are deep, strange, and typically never left Japan. They have huge rosters, career modes, edit-a-wrestler systems, and menus full of text few could read. I obsessively played Super Fire Pro Wrestling X Premium, created hundreds of wrestlers, won every title. That wouldn't have been possible without amazing hackers like Sydra and Phil along with the rest of the Fire Pro Wrestling community.

Now I'm giving back. My goal is to translate as many of the unique and interesting puroresu games as I can. My work will always be free and open source. I want nothing in return except for others to enjoy the games.

## Contact

The best way to reach me is an issue on the relevant GitHub repository, ideally with a screenshot, the patch version you used and where in the game you were.
